<Steps>
  <Step title="Read the Competition Rules">
    Start with the [official ARC Prize 2026 competition](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/arc-prize-2026-arc-agi-3) on Kaggle.
    That's where you'll find the rules, timeline, leaderboard, example submissions, and the discussion forum.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Understand the Task">
    Read the [Theory Guide](/theory/intro-to-games) to build a clear mental model of ARC-AGI-3 before writing your agent.
    Learn how environments work, what information your agent receives, which actions it can take, and how its performance is evaluated.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Explore the Tools">
    Get familiar with the tools available for building and testing ARC-AGI-3 agents.
    See [Tools Overview](/tools/tools-overview) for a walkthrough of official development utilities.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Build, Iterate, and Feel the AGI"> 
    Start building your agents locally, test them, and submit them to the competition as you make progress.
    The rest is up to you. Good luck.
  </Step>
</Steps>
